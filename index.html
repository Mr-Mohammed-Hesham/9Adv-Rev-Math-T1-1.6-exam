📐 القواعد الأساسية

Proportion: a/b = c/d
Cross Multiply: a × d = b × c
Solve for x: x = (b × c) / d
Check: Substitute back to verify
مثال: x/45 = 15/25
25x = 45 × 15 → 25x = 675 → x = 27
